Change log (optimised on image.tif only; no other image accessed; image.tif never modified)
1. Inspected image: 1022x1024 uint16, 12-bit. Two halves differ in contrast/brightness with a stitching seam at column 512 (median step ~458 counts vs ~2 typical). Endothelial cells low contrast; cancer cells round with bright/dark rims.
2. v1 preprocessing: per-side background flattening (median 60px + gaussian 32px, subtracted), Gaussian sigma 1, 1-99 percentile normalisation. First exploratory attempt flattened across the seam and produced a smeared seam artefact -> replaced by per-side flattening with automatic seam detection.
3. Cellpose-SAM (cpsam, CPU), diameter 55 px (~36 um, range 25-50 um), flow_threshold 0.4, cellprob_threshold -1, min_size 300. Diameter 50 vs 70 gave identical counts (467) in the exploratory run; kept 55. Run takes ~3-5 min on CPU.
4. Cancer-cell removal v1 (ring contrast / solidity / aspect on Cellpose objects) removed only 5 objects -> rejected. Size-only rule rejected (small endothelial cells wedged between cancer cells; some cancer cells merged into larger objects). Gradient-threshold detector missed low-contrast cancer cells -> rejected.
5. Cancer-cell removal v2 (final): fine-scale texture energy (DoG 1/4 px, RMS smoothed sigma 3), threshold 0.75 x per-side p90, close/fill/open, drop <250 px; Cellpose objects dropped if >=50% inside mask, or >=25% inside and < 1150 px (smaller than a 25 um cell). Visual check: most cancer cells on the right half removed; several faint cancer cells on the left half remain (see report).
6. Output: uint16 labels on original grid, CSV with area_um2 (0.65 um/px), aspect ratio (major/minor of fitted ellipse), flags touches_image_border, touches_cancer_cell. Replaced pandas with csv module; use skimage axis_*_length names.
7. Final run on image.tif (python pipeline.py image.tif labels.tif measurements.csv): 496 Cellpose objects, 131 removed as cancer/debris, 365 endothelial labels (47 touch image border, 182 touch a cancer cell). Area median 733 um2 (5-95%: 363-1226), aspect ratio median 1.46. No ground truth available, so validation was visual only.
